**Video Technology**

### Semantic Video Search

Searchable video content and transcripts

A video indexing and search system that makes large Video libraries searchable beyond titles and tags. It enables users to find relevant videos by searching for words and phrases spoken within the recordings.

- Indexes video metadata, including titles, tags, descriptions and creation dates
- Makes spoken words and phrases searchable within video content
- Combines metadata and transcript indexing to improve video discovery

**Engineering & Construction**

### Drawing Metadata Extractor

AI-powered document data extraction

A desktop document-processing system that extracts title block information from architectural and engineering drawings in PDF and image formats, then populates a cloud-based database automatically.

- Detects title blocks across drawings with different layouts, positions and orientations
- Extracts drawing metadata, including titles, authors, scales, versions and dates
- Automatically transfers extracted information into a centralized cloud database

### Destination Discovery Engine

Location-based travel discovery

A destination discovery platform that helps travellers explore places worth visiting in any searched location. It collects and organizes destination information from across the web and presents it through a mobile-friendly experience.

**Human Resource**

### Support Knowledge Extractor

AI-powered support knowledge management

An intelligent knowledge extraction system that turns millions of customer-support email threads into a structured FAQ knowledge base — helping support teams capture recurring questions and publish clear answers on the company website.

- Processes large volumes of email conversations to identify frequently asked questions
- Extracts and consolidates recurring questions from back-and-forth support threads
- Produces structured FAQ content ready for publication on the organization's website
